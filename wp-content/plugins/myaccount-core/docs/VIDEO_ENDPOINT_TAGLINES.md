# Video — Short taglines per endpoint

Use as captions, titles, or callouts in your video to highlight each screen.

## Gig description

Short copy for your gig listing (e.g. Fiverr, Upwork). Not for video taglines.

- Works with most WordPress themes, including popular ones (e.g. Astra, Kadence, Bricks, GeneratePress, OceanWP, and other theme builders).

---

## Login / Sign In
- **Welcome back** — Single-page sign-in with a focused, clutter-free layout.
- **Sign in** — Email and password with optional “keep me signed in.”
- **Inline validation** — Field-level errors before submit; no full-page reload.

---

## Sign Up / Create Account
- **Join us** — Account creation on the same page as login for a unified flow.
- **Create Account** — First name, last name, email, and password with clear requirements.
- **Password requirements** — Visible rules and strength guidance for secure signup.
- **Terms and Privacy** — Required consent with links to Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

---

## Forgot Password
- **Forgot password?** — Email-based reset; one field, one action.
- **Enter your email** — Reset instructions delivered to the provided address.

---

## Navigation
- **My Account menu** — Single control for Orders, Addresses, Payment methods, and Edit account.
- **Responsive dropdown** — Collapsible on small screens; closes on outside click for clear focus.

---

## Orders (Order History)
- **Order History** — View and track all orders in one place.
- **Card-based list** — One card per order with product thumbnail and “+N more” for multiple items.
- **Pagination** — Previous / Next with page and total count (e.g. “Page X of Y · Z orders”).

---

## View Order (Order details)
- **Order #123** — Full status, line items, and store updates.
- **Three-step timeline** — Placed → Processing → Complete with progress indication.
- **Estimated delivery** — Optional expected delivery date when configured.
- **Items and summary** — Line items, totals, shipping and billing addresses, payment method.
- **Order updates** — Chronological notes from the store for transparency.

---

## Edit Account (My Info)
- **My Info** — Update personal and contact information in one form.
- **Personal and contact** — Name, read-only account email, and full billing address.
- **Change password** — Optional block on the same page; no separate screen.
- **Realtime validation** — Per-field errors and loading state on Save.

---

## Address Book
- **Address Book** — Central management of shipping and billing addresses.
- **Address cards** — Name, phone, default indicator; Edit, Set default, and Delete actions.
- **Add and edit in modal** — No navigation; modal closes on Escape or backdrop click.
- **Add Address** — Dedicated empty state with a single primary action to add.

---

## Payment Methods
- **Payment Methods** — View and manage saved payment methods.
- **Add Payment Method** — In-page form to add a new card or method.
- **Saved methods** — Brand, last four digits, Set default and Delete per method.
- **Tokenization support** — Compatible with gateways that support saved methods (e.g. Stripe, PayPal).

---

## Add Payment Method (dedicated page)
- **Add payment method** — Dedicated flow with the same styling as the main Payment Methods page.

---

## Reset Password
- **Reset password** — Set a new password after following the email link.
- **New password and confirm** — Validation for match and minimum strength.

---

## Overview (intro/outro)
- **Modern My Account** — Alpine.js, inline validation, modals, and responsive layout.
- **Single plugin, full coverage** — Login, Orders, Addresses, Payment methods, Edit account.
- **Mobile-first** — Dropdown navigation, compact forms, and touch-optimized controls.

## Full script (product demo)

Full voice-over script per screen — customer-focused, conversion-oriented. No technical jargon.

---

### Intro

*"This is My Account — everything your customers need in one place: sign in, view orders, manage addresses, payment methods, and update their details. The layout is clear and easy to use on both desktop and mobile. Let me walk you through each part."*

---

### Login

*"Signing in is quick: one page, email and password, and you're in. Customers can tick 'Remember me' so they don't have to enter details next time. If something's missing or wrong, the page tells them right away so they can fix it without guessing. No account yet? They can switch to the tab next to it and create one there."*

---

### Sign Up

*"Creating an account takes just a few fields: name, email, and password. The page guides them to choose a strong password so their account stays secure. At the end they tick to agree to the terms and privacy policy — there are links if they want to read them. Once they're done, one click and they can start shopping."*

---

### Forgot Password

*"Forgotten password is no hassle: they enter the email they signed up with and click send. We send a reset link to that email. One step, no fuss — they get back into their account quickly."*

---

### Navigation

*"From this menu they can go anywhere: Orders, Addresses, Payment methods, Edit account. On mobile the menu stays compact — tap to open, pick where to go, then close. Simple."*

---

### Orders (Order History)

*"All their orders are right here. Each order is a card with product images so they can see at a glance what they bought. If there are many orders, they move through pages with clear Previous / Next and page numbers. Tap any order to see the details."*

---

### View Order (Order details)

*"This is the full picture for each order: status, from placed to processing to complete, and when we have it, an expected delivery date. Below that are the items, total, shipping and billing address, and how they paid. Any updates we add — for example when the order has shipped — show here too so they're always in the loop."*

---

### Edit Account (My Info)

*"They update their personal details and billing address on one page. When they're done, they hit Save and it's updated. If they want to change their password, they can do it right here — no need to go somewhere else. Everything in one place so they can update quickly without getting lost."*

---

### Address Book

*"The address book lets them manage shipping and billing addresses. Each address is a card with name, phone, and a default marker. They can edit, set default, or remove an address. To add or edit, a small window opens right on the page — no leaving the page — and they close it when they're done. No addresses yet? One tap on Add address and they're started."*

---

### Payment Methods

*"This page shows all saved cards or payment methods: card type and last four digits. They can set a default or remove one they no longer use. Adding a new card is done right here or on a dedicated page — details are stored securely so checkout is faster next time."*

---

### Add Payment Method (dedicated page)

*"This page is dedicated to adding a card or payment method. Same simple flow as on the main page: enter the details and save. Handy when they want to add a card without going through other steps."*

---

### Reset Password

*"After they click the link in the email, they land here to set a new password. They enter it twice so we can confirm there are no typos. The page suggests a strong password. Then they hit the button — straightforward and secure."*

---

### Outro

*"So that's My Account: sign in, sign up, forgot password, orders, order details, edit account, address book, and payment methods — all in one place and easy to use on desktop and phone. Thanks for watching."*
